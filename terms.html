5. Invitation expiry

Invitations are published for a fixed duration chosen at purchase: 1, 3, 7, 15, 30, 90 or 365 days, as listed on the Pricing page. The period starts when the invitation is published, not when the event happens.

When the period ends the link stops displaying the invitation and shows a notice that the event has passed, and uploaded images are removed from storage. Expiry is automatic, so please extend before the date if you want the page to remain available. We may send a reminder before expiry, but you should not rely on receiving one.
