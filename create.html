    Questions about payment

    Is it a one-time payment?

    Yes. ₹99 is charged once. There is no subscription, and nothing renews automatically.

    How long will my invitation stay online?

    Until 31 days after your event. After that the card and host page are deleted automatically, so download a copy if you want to keep it.

    What if I close the payment window or the payment fails?

    Nothing is charged. Your invitation stays saved, and you can try again anytime.

    I paid, but my invitation didn't go live. What now?

    Your payment is safe and remembered in this browser. Click Host My Invitation again: it publishes without asking you to pay a second time. If it still fails, contact us and we'll fix it or refund you.

    Can I get a refund?

    Hosting is delivered as soon as you pay, so payments are generally non-refundable. If it fails because of us (the invitation doesn't publish or the link doesn't work), we'll fix it or refund you. Duplicate charges are always refunded. See our Terms.

    Which email gets the confirmation?

    The email address you entered in the Details step. Check your Spam/Junk folder if you don't see it.
