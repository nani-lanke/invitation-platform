Getting started

A digital invitation is a web page instead of a printed card. It carries your names, the date, the venue, your photos and a live countdown, and it lives at its own link. Guests tap the link and it opens in whatever browser they already have — nothing to install, nothing to download and no account to create.

Open the Create Invitation page and work through five short steps: choose the occasion, fill in your event details, add photos if you want them, pick colours and fonts, then preview. The card on the right updates as you type, so you always know exactly what you are sending.

In the hosted version, yes — each invitation gets its own link and its own plan, which is how families handle a wedding, a reception and a house warming in the same season. In this demonstration build your browser holds one draft at a time; starting over replaces it.

Never. Guests only ever click a link. No sign-up, no app and no login screen stands between them and your invitation — which matters a lot when you are inviting relatives who are not comfortable with new apps.

Photos, music and maps

Absolutely, and we would encourage it — a real photograph of the couple or the baby does more for an invitation than any stock image. You are responsible for having the right to use whatever you upload, which is covered in the Terms & Conditions.

You can choose a background track in the editor today, and playback arrives with the hosted version. When it does, music will always start muted with a visible control — guests opening an invitation in a quiet office should never be ambushed by shehnai.

Yes. Paste a Google Maps share link into the editor, or just type the full address and we will build the link for you. Guests get a Directions button that opens the venue in their own maps app with navigation ready to start.

Sharing and guests

Mobile is the primary target. Every layout is designed at 320px first and then widened, and the whole page weighs a few hundred kilobytes, so it opens quickly even on a weak connection or an older phone.

Plans, expiry and editing

For exactly the duration you choose — one, three, seven, fifteen, thirty, ninety or three hundred and sixty-five days. All seven options are listed on the Pricing page.

The link stops showing the invitation and displays a short notice that the event has passed. Your uploaded photos are then removed from storage. If you want to keep the page as a memento, choose a longer plan or extend before it lapses.
