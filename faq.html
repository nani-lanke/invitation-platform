Getting started

A digital invitation is a web page instead of a printed card. It carries your names, the date, the venue, your photos and a live countdown, and it lives at its own link. Guests tap the link and it opens in whatever browser they already have — nothing to install, nothing to download and no account to create.

Open the Create Invitation page and work through seven short steps: choose the occasion, fill in your event details, add the programme and header wording, add photos or music if you want them, pick a design, colours and fonts, then download your invitation free — or host it online for ₹99. The card beside the form updates as you type, so you always know exactly what you are sending.

Plans, expiry and editing

Until 31 days after your event. The ₹99 hosting plan is a one-time payment; the invitation stays live through the event and for 31 days after it. If you did not set an event date, the 31 days count from the day you hosted it. See the Pricing page for details.

31 days after the event, the card page, the host page and their uploaded photos and music are deleted automatically, and the link shows that the invitation has ended. Download the card or page before then if you want to keep a copy. If you would like it taken down sooner, write to us from the email address you hosted it with and include the link.

Before you host it, edit as much as you like — the draft is saved in your browser and the card updates as you type. A hosted invitation is published exactly as it was when you paid, so check names, the date and the venue carefully first. If something changes later, create the corrected invitation and host it at a new link.
